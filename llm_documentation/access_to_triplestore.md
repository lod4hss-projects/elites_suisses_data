## SPARQL Endpoint

[3 March 2026]

For the moment being the Elites Suisses Knowledge Graph is available on this SPARQL Endpoint: https://swiss-elites.lod4hss.cloud/wisski/endpoint/default

Only GET queries are allowed.

The Knwledge Graph triples are to be found in this RDF graph: https://swiss-elites.lod4hss.cloud/resource/

Other RDF graphs are available but they are needed by Wisski and not to be considered.
